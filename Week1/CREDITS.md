# Test clip credits

## ESC-50 (current evaluation)

`eval/run_eval.py` scores 560 clips from ESC-50: Karol J. Piczak, "ESC: Dataset for Environmental Sound
Classification", ACM Multimedia 2015, https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50. The clips are CC BY-NC 4.0 (individual
clips come from freesound.org under their own CC licenses) and are not redistributed here;
`eval/fetch_esc50.py` downloads them. Non-commercial use only.
