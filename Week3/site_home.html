How it goes

  1. Gemma is looking 48 s

    From a spot you can find again. Gemma lists what's out of place as it spots it.

  2. plastic bottles floor cardboard box floor black umbrella floor plastic bags floor

    2Untick what isn't there

    Small models imagine things. You fix the list before anything is planned.

  3. 90 secondssome

    3Say how much energy

    Ten seconds to ten minutes. The planner picks the best moves for exactly that.

  4. Plastic bottlesfloor → bin, about 30 sStart

    4Do one move

    One card, one timer. It learns how long things take you, and an after photo checks the result.

Your room stays on your computer

Gemma 3 4B runs through Ollama on your own machine. Photos stay in memory, lose their GPS data before the model sees them, and are never saved. Nothing leaves the computer.
