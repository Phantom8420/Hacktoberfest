How it works

  1. It reads your screenEvery few seconds while you work, it reads the text on screen and keeps a small screenshot.
  2. It keeps it on your PCEncrypted, with secrets blacked out and private windows skipped. Pause or forget any time.
  3. You askType or say what you saw. It answers in one line and circles the screenshot it came from.
