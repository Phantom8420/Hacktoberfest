Everything below works in the inspector further down this page. Try the demo floor if you don't have a tile to tap yet.

Open the inspector

  1. Tap

    Drop a coin through a pen tube onto each tile, row by row. Thud finds every tap and measures its pitch and ring.

  2. Confirm

    Thud points at the odd-sounding tiles first. Check a few by hand and mark two solid and two hollow.

  3. Report

    TabPFN scores the rest of the floor. Add photos and Gemma writes the report, sealed with a receipt.

What it can't tell you

  • It needs your confirmationsUntil you mark two solid and two hollow tiles, Thud only ranks tiles by how unusual they sound.
  • Not yet field testedThe pipeline is checked on synthetic taps, not on a measured set of real hollow tiles.
  • Noise hurtsDrills, traffic and voices make extra taps or hide real ones. Tap in a quiet room.
  • Receipts are tamper-evident, not tamper-proofSend the head hash to someone else, and a rewritten chain no longer matches it.

Try it on your own floor

Thud is open source and runs on a laptop with no GPU. Install it, start it, and open it on the same machine.

pip install -r requirements.txt
ollama pull gemma3:4b
python -m thud serve
